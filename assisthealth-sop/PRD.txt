Introduction & Purpose: State the problem you are solving and for whom. Clearly define the business objectives and how the product aligns with the company's strategic goals.
Target Audience & User Personas: Detail the specific user segments the product will serve. Outline their needs, pain points, and motivations.
Features & Functionality: Break down the product's capabilities into specific features. Prioritize these features using Must-have, Should-have, Could-have, Won't-have (MoSCoW) to guide development efforts.
User Flow & Design: Include wireframes, mockups, or user flow diagrams to provide visual context for how users will interact with the product.
System & Technical Requirements: Specify non-functional requirements, such as performance benchmarks (e.g., page load times), security standards, scalability needs, and regulatory compliance obligations.
Assumptions and Constraints: Document any assumptions made during planning, technical or business constraints.
Risks & Dependencies: Identify potential risks to the project and document any dependencies on other teams or projects.
Success Metrics & Release Criteria: Define how you will measure the product's success. Outline the key performance indicators (KPIs) and the criteria that must be met for the product to be released. These might include functionality, usability, and performance standards.
Timeline & Release Plan: Provide a high-level roadmap with key milestones and estimated release dates. This helps manage expectations and coordinates efforts across teams.
Stakeholder Review & Approval: List all key stakeholders and document their review and sign-off for universal buy-in before development starts.
